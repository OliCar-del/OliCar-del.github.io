{%- comment -%} One line of _data/cv.yml: plain text, or `text` linked to its `project` or `url`, where `link` picks the words that become the link (default: all of the text). Usage: {% include cv-text.html line=point %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.line.text -%} {%- include cv-url.html line=include.line -%} {%- if cv_url -%} {%- assign cv_words = include.line.link | default: include.line.text -%} {%- capture cv_anchor %}{{ cv_words }}{% endcapture -%} {{- include.line.text | replace_first: cv_words, cv_anchor -}} {%- else -%} {{- include.line.text -}} {%- endif -%} {%- else -%} {{- include.line -}} {%- endif -%}
